# Verification Report: 1.2.3 — Document upload and parsing

**Item ID:** 1.2.3
**Description:** Document upload and parsing (PDF, Word/PPTX, Markdown, text)
**Date:** 2026-06-12
**Verdict:** ⚠️ WARN

## Team Review (Architecture & SRE)
- **Max (SRE / Security)**: Parsing complex binaries in the Node.js event loop or even `worker_threads` is a memory leak and DoS risk waiting to happen. Segfaults in C++ bindings will crash the host.
- **Leo (Architect)**: Agreed. Document parsing will be strictly offloaded to isolated OS processes using `child_process.fork()` or `spawn`. We will enforce `--max-old-space-size` and hard OS-level timeouts to kill rogue parsers cleanly.
- **Conclusion**: Architecture mandated change (Process Isolation).
