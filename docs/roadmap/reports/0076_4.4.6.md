# Verification Report: 4.4.6 — Schema: contentHash, affiliatedAt columns on documents

**Item ID:** 4.4.6
**Description:** Schema: contentHash, affiliatedAt columns on documents
**Date:** 2026-06-13
**Verdict:** ⚠️ WARN

## Team Review (Architecture & SRE)
- **Leo (Architect)**: Adding `contentHash` and `affiliatedAt` to track document uniqueness and lifecycle transitions.
- **Max (SRE / Security)**: If `contentHash` isn't indexed, deduplication checks at upload will cause full table scans. Make sure a `UNIQUE INDEX` (or at least a standard index) is placed on `contentHash`, otherwise it's useless for fast deduplication.
- **Conclusion**: Performance mandated schema change (Index on contentHash).
