**Date:** 2026-06-13
**Verdict:** ⚠️ WARN

## Team Review (Architecture & SRE)
- **Max (SRE / Security)**: If I see `drizzle-kit push` running anywhere near a production environment, I will revoke your deploy access immediately. `push` is destructive.
- **Leo (Architect)**: `drizzle-kit push` will be strictly documented and gated for local development only. Production must use `drizzle-kit migrate`, executed as a pre-flight step in our CI/CD pipeline.
- **Conclusion**: Architecture mandated change (Strict migration segregation).
