## Team Review (Architecture & SRE)
- **Max (SRE / Security)**: Unbounded JSONB arrays for `sourceCommits` will cause catastrophic PostgreSQL TOAST bloat. A naive junction table will also cause explosive combinatorics.
- **Leo (Architect)**: Agreed. We will discard the generic JSONB approach and naive junction tables. We will strictly use Temporal Range Anchors (tracking `introduced_in_commit` and `verified_until_commit` columns on knowledge nodes).
- **Conclusion**: Architecture mandated schema change.
