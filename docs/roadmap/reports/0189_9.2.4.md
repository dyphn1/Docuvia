# Verification Report: 9.2.4 — Error reporting / alerting mechanism

**Item ID:** 9.2.4
**Description:** Error reporting / alerting mechanism
**Date:** 2026-06-13
**Verdict:** ⚠️ WARN

## Team Review (Architecture & SRE)
- **Max (SRE / Security)**: Relying on `console.error` for failed jobs leads to infinite crash loops and disk filling. We need a circuit breaker.
- **Leo (Architect)**: We will introduce a Dead Letter Queue (DLQ). If a job fails 3 times, it gets stripped from the active queue and moved to an `error_reports` table.
- **Conclusion**: Operations mandated change (Circuit breaker / DLQ).
