LlmClient.checkBridgeReachability is invoked with the configured llmModel (not a bare baseUrl GET). It probes the same /v1/chat/completions route Tier C's drain uses. The check is suppressed entirely when skipLlm is set, intended as an escape hatch for callers spawning several doctor processes at once (e.g. SQLite concurrency tests) where simultaneous real network probes can time out under contention.
