System One rank scores are ordinal. Calibration-quality ECE/Brier are computed only after fitting a Beta(1,1)-smoothed isotonic map from selected complementary-fold rows. Deterministic folds keep each duplicateGroup entirely in one held fold; its labels are excluded from both that fold’s threshold selection and probability-map fit. Abstentions receive no probability but remain in all-eligible coverage and abstention denominators. Selected unscorable or conflicting labels count as failures, with scorable-only metrics reported separately. Call-shape maps are diagnostic sensitivities with explicit minimum support; they do not alter selection or production behavior. These are in-split, two-family estimates, not production confidence, Tier B skip authority, or unseen certification.
