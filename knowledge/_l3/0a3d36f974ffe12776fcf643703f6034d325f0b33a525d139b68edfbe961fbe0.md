The Agent Hooks Check probes two specific repo-relative paths: <root>/.claude/hooks/docuvia-hook.js and <root>/.cursor/hooks/docuvia-hook.cjs. A missing hook file reports PASS with 'not found (run `docuvia init` to install)' rather than FAIL -- not selecting a platform at init is treated as a legitimate state. The check is skipped entirely (no fs.stat calls, no diagnostic key) when skipHooks is set.
