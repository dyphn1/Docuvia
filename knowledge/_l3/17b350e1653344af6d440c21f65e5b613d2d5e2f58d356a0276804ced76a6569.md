The shrink-guard logic in `hydrate()` exempts small graphs (below `MIN_NODES_FOR_SHRINK_GUARD`) from refusal, even if the incoming graph is empty. This ensures test-scale or early-stage graphs are not blocked by the guard, which is designed to protect large, established graphs from accidental truncation.
