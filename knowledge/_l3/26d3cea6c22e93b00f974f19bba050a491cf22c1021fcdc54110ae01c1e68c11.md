Introduced `IGraphStore.withTransaction()` and `IGraphNodesRepo.withFtsSyncSuspended()` to ensure atomic persistence and suspend FTS5 re-tokenization during bulk inserts. This prevents partial state corruption on disk I/O failures and mitigates unbounded WAL growth during large-scale ingestion (e.g., vscode's 293k+ nodes), as verified by a successful full ingestion with PRAGMA integrity_check.
