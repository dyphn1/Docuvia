The `persistFileAndSymbolNodes` operation is wrapped in `store.graph.withFtsSyncSuspended()`, which disables FTS5 tokenization triggers during bulk inserts. This addresses a performance bottleneck where per-row FTS updates caused unbounded WAL growth and I/O errors at scale (e.g., 293k+ nodes). The suspension ensures FTS sync occurs only after all nodes are inserted, reducing overhead to a single sync pass.
