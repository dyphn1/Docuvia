The service exposes this through an explicit measurement method; normal hypothesis calls retain the same result. This changes no candidate policy, ranking, calibration, production resolution, Tier B scheduling, or persistence behavior. The TRAIN audit is bound to pinned v4 source and TRAIN labels and is descriptive evidence only.
