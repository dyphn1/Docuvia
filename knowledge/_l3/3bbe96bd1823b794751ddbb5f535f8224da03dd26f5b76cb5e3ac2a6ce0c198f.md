ImpactWorkflow resolves HEAD-sha freshness once via the shared resolveGraphFreshness util (extracted from StatusWorkflow, #193). When lastIngestedSourceSha differs from HEAD the result carries an additive graphFreshness {state: stale, graphSourceSha, headSha} and the first rung of resolveImpactEpistemic makes it lower-bound with RISK_NOTE_GRAPH_STALE, for empty and non-empty results alike (empty stays UNKNOWN, non-empty keeps its band). Fresh and fail-open unknown freshness are omitted, matching status. A dirty working tree is not staleness (PLAT-007; follow-up #523).
