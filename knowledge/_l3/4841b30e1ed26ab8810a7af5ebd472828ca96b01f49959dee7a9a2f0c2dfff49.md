Every diagnostic method (e.g., runDbDiagnostics, runGitDiagnostics, runGraphHealthDiagnostics) populates the diagnostics map with a DiagnosticResult object that includes a status field (DiagnosticStatus.PASS or DiagnosticStatus.FAIL) and optional message/suggestion strings. The status enum drives the final allPassed calculation via results.every(Boolean).
