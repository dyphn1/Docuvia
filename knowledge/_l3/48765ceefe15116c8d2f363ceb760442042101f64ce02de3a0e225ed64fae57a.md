The commit introduces two distinct counters—`external` for node_modules, node builtins, and ambient globals and `unknownReceiver` for member calls with invisible receivers—rather than merging them into the existing `unresolvable` metric, ensuring the raw counts stay stamped in meta and remain derivable while accurately reflecting resolver performance.
