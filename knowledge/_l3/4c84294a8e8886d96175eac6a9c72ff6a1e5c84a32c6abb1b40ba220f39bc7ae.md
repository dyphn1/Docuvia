The `hydrate()` method ensures that both L2 graph data (nodes/edges) and L3 card imports are performed within a single write-locked transaction via `store.withWriteLock()`. This guarantees atomicity: either both the graph and cards are loaded, or neither is, preventing partial or inconsistent hydration states.
