GitMessages includes paired messages for doctor --fix operations: REPAIRED_DUPLICATE_POST_COMMIT_HOOK and NOTHING_TO_REPAIR. This pattern ensures that every repair action has a corresponding 'nothing to do' message, providing clear feedback to users about whether intervention was actually needed or performed.
