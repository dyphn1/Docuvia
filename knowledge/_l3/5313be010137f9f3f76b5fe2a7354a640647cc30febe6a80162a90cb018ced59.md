The `persistLocked` method wraps the entire persistence operation in `store.withTransaction()`, ensuring all database operations (node/link inserts, deletions) are executed as a single atomic transaction. This decision restores the original behavior from `SqliteGraphRepository.persistAstGraph()` and addresses a critical performance issue: at scale (e.g., vscode-repo), autocommit mode (one fsync per row) caused unbounded WAL growth and eventual I/O failures. The transaction reduces fsyncs to a single BEGIN/COMMIT pair, enabling practical completion of large-scale persists.
