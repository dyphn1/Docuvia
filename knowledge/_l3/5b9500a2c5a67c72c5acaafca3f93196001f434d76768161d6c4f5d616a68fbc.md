GitMessages includes specific upgrade notification strings (UPGRADED_LEGACY_POST_COMMIT_HOOK, UPGRADED_LEGACY_PRE_PUSH_HOOK) that describe the exact transformation applied during in-place hook upgrades. These messages document the migration from 'docuvia snapshot' to 'docuvia analyze' for post-commit hooks, and the addition of the sync-knowledge step for pre-push hooks, providing auditability of hook evolution.
