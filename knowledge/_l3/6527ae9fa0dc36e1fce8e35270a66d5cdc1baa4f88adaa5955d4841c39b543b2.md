The accumulation of `unresolvable`, `external`, and `unknownReceiver` uses `(x ?? 0)` nullish-coalescing guards, while `total`, `resolved`, `selfDiscarded`, and `unresolved` use plain `+=`. This indicates the author treats `unresolvable`, `external`, and `unknownReceiver` as potentially-undefined fields in some `CallResolutionStats` records, falling back to 0, whereas the other fields are assumed always present as numbers.
