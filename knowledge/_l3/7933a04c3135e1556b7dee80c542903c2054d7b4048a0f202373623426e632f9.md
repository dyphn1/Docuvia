When a check is skipped (via the skip flag in runOrSkip), the method returns true, effectively marking that particular diagnostic as passed. This means the caller cannot fail due to an intentionally omitted check—aligning with the design principle that skipping is a legitimate operational state (e.g., skipDb=false is default, allowing users to disable DB-specific checks).
