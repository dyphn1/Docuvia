The watchdog only increments its streak counter for batches with zero progress from healthy (non-degraded) language buckets. Fully degraded batches (all languages unavailable) do not count toward the streak because they indicate environmental issues, not file-specific stagnation. Progress (even partial) resets the streak to zero.
