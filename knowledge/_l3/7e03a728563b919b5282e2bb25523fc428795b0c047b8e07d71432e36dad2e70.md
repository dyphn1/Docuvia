The architecture maintains three parallel records — `CLI_COMMANDS` (string values), `CLI_COMMAND_DESCRIPTIONS` (descriptions), and `CLI_COMMAND_FLAGS` (flag arrays) — all keyed by the same `CliCommand` type. This allows future commands to be added as one entry in each record without restructuring dispatch.
