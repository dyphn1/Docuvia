The strict proof dispatcher leaves existing direct-this and named-import paths intact, then routes supported explicit receiver forms to separately versioned Q3 rules. Q3 proof targets do not alter ScopeResolver attribution, heuristic candidate selection, Tier B execution, or calibration labels.
