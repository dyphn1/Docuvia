The Call Graph Resolution Check always reports PASS but carries a 'low resolution rate' note and a suggestion pointing to `docuvia analyze --escalate-to-lsp --full` when the rate (resolved / (total - selfDiscarded)) falls below the threshold. Self-discarded calls are excluded from the denominator. When no resolution counters were ever stamped into meta, the check reports an informational no-data PASS.
