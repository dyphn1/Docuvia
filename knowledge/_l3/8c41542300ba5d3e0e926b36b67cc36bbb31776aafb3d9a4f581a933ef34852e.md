patternCouldMatchTarget matches a target by its extension-less stem or its NodeNext runtime spelling: .ts/.tsx/.js/.jsx -> .js, .mts/.mjs -> .mjs, .cts/.cjs -> .cjs (RUNTIME_EXTENSION_BY_SOURCE_EXTENSION), in both literal and interpolated specifiers. A .js suffix never admits an .mts source.
