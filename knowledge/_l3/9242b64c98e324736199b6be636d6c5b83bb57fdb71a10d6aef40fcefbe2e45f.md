The Q2 rule accepts explicit named imports whose in-repository export chain resolves to one supported program function. It traces by (file, symbol), fingerprints the caller, every chain file, and relevant path configuration, and abstains on cycles, depth overflow, dead ends, incomplete facts, ambiguity, truncation, workspace escape, type-only or namespace exports, and final name collisions. Projection uses the proof target only; ScopeResolver remains responsible for caller attribution.
