A non-empty Tier C queue is reported FAIL only when analyze.log shows no tierC.summary event (no drain has ever completed) OR the most recent summary processed 0 items. A queue plus a recent summary that processed >0 items is reported PASS regardless of pending count. Failure message surfaces the most recent item_failed reason (e.g. 'bridge-unreachable') plus the pending count.
