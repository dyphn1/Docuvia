The Q3 receiver proof module adds separately versioned, source-bound proofs for super calls, inherited this calls, explicitly typed receivers, and new-expression receivers. Proofs require unique complete class/member inventories, retain ScopeResolver caller attribution, and fingerprint every consulted class, alias, base, and import source so invalidation removes stale results. Ambiguous or unsupported type forms abstain; the rules do not promote calibration or skip Tier B.
