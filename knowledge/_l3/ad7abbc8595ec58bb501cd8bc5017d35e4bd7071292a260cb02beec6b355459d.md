The `isStale()` method determines staleness by comparing the store's recorded hydration SHA (from `META_KEY_KNOWLEDGE_TIP_SHA`) with the currently resolved knowledge commit SHA. If they differ, the store is considered stale. An empty store (no recorded SHA) is not treated as stale if no knowledge branch exists.
