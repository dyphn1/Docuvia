The DoctorWorkflow.execute() method returns an object containing two fields: 'allPassed' (boolean indicating whether every diagnostic check passed) and 'diagnostics' (Record<string, DiagnosticResult> mapping diagnostic keys to their result objects). This follows the pattern established in the codebase where each diagnostic check populates the diagnostics map, and the final result aggregates these outcomes.
