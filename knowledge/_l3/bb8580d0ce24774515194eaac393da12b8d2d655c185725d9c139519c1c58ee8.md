Files in degraded language buckets are excluded from zero-progress watchdog consideration because they represent environmental signals (provider unavailable) rather than stuck-files signals. This prevents misattributing environment-related degradation to file-specific issues, as established in issue #33 to avoid escalating files to permanent failure due to transient provider problems.
