persistDynamicDependencyEvidence re-runs resolveCandidates for every retained record (records whose loader was not re-parsed in this batch) against the current project_files list. Expression and literal prefix/suffix are persisted and 'interpolated' is derived by scannedFromEvidence(), so no source re-read is needed. A newly added candidate joins the bounded set and growth past MAX_BOUNDED_CANDIDATES (64) moves the record to candidate-set-exceeds-64 instead of leaving a stale bounded set.
