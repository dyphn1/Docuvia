The denominator subtracts four specific structural limits from the total: selfDiscarded (issue #221), unresolvable (issue #192), external (issue #230), and unknownReceiver (issue #230). These are treated as inherent limitations of the analysis, not resolution failures, and are excluded to reflect only the call sites that name-based resolution is actually expected to handle.
