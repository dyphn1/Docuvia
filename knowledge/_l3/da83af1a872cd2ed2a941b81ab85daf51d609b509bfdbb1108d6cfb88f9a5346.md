The `markSynced()` method updates the hydration state atomically by recording the resolved knowledge-tip SHA in `store.meta` without modifying the graph. This ensures the staleness check (`isStale()`) remains consistent with the actual hydration state, even if the graph was populated by other means (e.g., `init`).
