For each parsed file, `persistFileAndSymbolNodes` explicitly deletes stale nodes (and their outgoing links/tag-links) via `store.graph.deleteNodesForPath(result.file)` before inserting new nodes. This ensures old graph state does not linger after re-parsing, maintaining consistency without requiring manual cleanup.
