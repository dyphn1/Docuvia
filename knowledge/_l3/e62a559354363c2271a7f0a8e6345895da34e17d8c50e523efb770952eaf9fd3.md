When the zero-progress watchdog streak reaches GitConstants.DEFAULT_TIER_B_ZERO_PROGRESS_MAX_BATCHES, it marks the current batch's zero-progress remainder from healthy buckets as permanently failed. These files are removed from retryable status (failedEntries) and added to permanentFailed to prevent repeated re-attempts in subsequent batches.
