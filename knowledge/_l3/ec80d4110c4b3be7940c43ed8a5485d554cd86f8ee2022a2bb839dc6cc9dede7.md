A source-index SHA marks the committed HEAD represented by the available persisted parser facts; it does not assert every source file parsed. Delta candidate-domain invalidation compares old and current candidate signatures for changed files and falls back conservatively when a changed file lacks old facts. Unchanged missing-fact files remain absent until their content changes, avoiding repeated full-inventory reparses.
