`registerResolverFiles` pre-registers all parsed files' imports and locals with `ScopeResolver` before linking edges. This ensures cross-file resolution (e.g., calls/extends) sees the entire batch of symbols, not just those from previously processed files, enabling accurate edge creation across the entire codebase.
